IMPORTANT PRODUCTION LIMITATION (BY DESIGN):
- If two customers must simultaneously pay the exact same amount (for example ₹500) into the same receiving account and the bank SMS does not carry a YEEDI order identifier, automatic unique attribution is impossible from amount/account/time alone. The collision guard therefore blocks the second overlapping ₹500 slot on that receiving account. Use multiple receiving accounts, unique payable amounts, or a stronger bank-side reference/API if high same-amount concurrency is required.
